## Game Overview

**Onryō** is a **Fast Arena Shooter** where speed, intensity, and constant pressure define the experience.  
Players take on the role of a vengeful spirit capable of **possessing enemies**, turning their own bodies and weapons against their allies.

Set in **feudal Japan**, the game blends **dark, macabre themes** with **slightly retro visuals**, creating a brutal and oppressive atmosphere designed for **midcore to hardcore players** seeking a fast-paced and demanding challenge.

<br>

## Core Concept

### Fast Arena Shooter

The Fast Arena Shooter genre is characterized by:

- Extremely fast-paced gameplay  
- High player tension with intense combat phases  
- First-person shooting mechanics  
- Clear win and loss conditions  

### Possession System (Core Mechanic)

The game introduces a **possession layer** that deeply impacts gameplay:

- Multiple weapon types  
- Health, ammunition, and consumable item management  
- Several character controllers depending on the possessed enemy  

In **Onryō**, the player starts as a **pure spirit**, unable to directly harm enemies.  
By possessing opponents, the player gains:

- A physical body  
- The enemy’s weapons  
- Their stats and inventory  

This mechanic allows players to strategically turn enemies against each other and adapt constantly to the arena.

## Narrative

The story follows a woman [**betrayed and murdered by her husband**](https://youtu.be/Z4lanWCPNc0), with the help of his loyal servant.  
Condemned to death, she returns as a vengeful spirit — an **Onryō** — seeking revenge against every man within the palace.

Her vengeance is executed from within:  
she possesses her enemies, forcing them to slaughter one another using their own bodies and weapons.

<br>

## Environmental Storytelling

The level design integrates **visual storytelling elements** to reinforce the narrative:

- A hidden chamber contains the woman’s preserved corpse, frozen in time as an easter egg, rewarding exploration with collectibles  
  - This represents **objective environmental storytelling**, confirming known narrative elements  

- Portraits of women scattered throughout the palace reveal the husband’s disturbing obsession, especially toward her  
  - This is **subjective environmental storytelling**, adding new narrative layers the player may not initially know  

- When the player moves through the level in spirit form, nearby lanterns flicker with a blue glow  
  - This creates a haunting, supernatural visual feedback and reinforces the eerie atmosphere  

<br>

## Artistic Direction

- Dark and oppressive environments  
- Subtle retro-inspired visuals  
- Strong contrast between light and shadow  
- Visual effects emphasizing the supernatural nature of the player  

The visual style is designed to support the **macabre tone** while maintaining clarity and readability in fast-paced combat.
